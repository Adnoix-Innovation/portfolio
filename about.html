About

More than a marketer.

I’m a Marketing Technologist. I build and connect the technology that powers marketing: websites, CRM platforms, tracking, APIs, automation and AI-assisted workflows.

At Delta Strike International, a laser tag manufacturer selling to 520+ venues worldwide, I was the main contributor to the company’s global website rebuild. I manage the HubSpot CRM integration and Google Ads conversion tracking, and I create collateral and content for international trade shows like IAAPA. That work grew genuine website traffic by 60% year-over-year, lifted engagement by 35%, and helped land a TVNZ Breakfast segment on the company.

I also run Pulsr Digital, a freelance web studio that has helped seven local businesses get online, including a pro bono build for the Handmade Studio community. I founded hey.kiwi to make getting online simpler and more affordable for sole traders.

I’ve completed a Bachelor of Digital Innovation at Yoobee Colleges (November 2026) and hold a Certificate in Creative Media, so I bring design skills too. My greatest strength, though, is the technical side of marketing: bridging the gap between marketing, technology and business.
