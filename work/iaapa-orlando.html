IAAPA Expo Orlando 2026: getting operators to the booth

IAAPA Expo is the attractions industry’s biggest trade show. Delta Strike had two booths there to meet family entertainment centre operators and distributors. I produced the campaign around it, from printed brochures and paid social before the show to live content from the show floor.

Role
Marketing Specialist: campaign, design, content, ads
When
Oct–Nov 2026 · show 18–20 Nov
Where
Orlando, Florida · Booths S4281 & S4278
Tools
Meta Ads Manager, Adobe CC, DaVinci Resolve, Instagram

The brief

Delta Strike sells high-ticket laser tag systems, arenas and attractions to venues worldwide. A trade show is one of the few times buyers are all in one place, so the goal was simple: get the right people to our booths, and give them something to take away.

  • Announce Delta Strike’s IAAPA presence and show deals before the expo
  • Drive booth visits from operators, distributors and investors
  • Create content during the show that keeps the brand visible
  • Produce print collateral for sales conversations at the booth and after

1 · Before: paid social

I ran a Meta campaign of seven ads in the lead-up to the show. Each ad spoke to a different buyer: new venue owners, operators looking for ROI, distributors, and investors needing finance. Every ad ended with the same action: visit our booth.

Results

Every ad beat Meta’s typical click-through rate of about 1–1.5%. The laser tag announcement was the stand-out: 327 clicks at 66 cents each.

2 · At the booth: print

I designed four product brochures for sales conversations at the booth and follow-up afterwards. They range from a 20-page deep dive on the Genesis phaser and vest to landscape spreads for Delta Putt arcade golf.

3 · During: live from the floor

Over the three days of the show I posted twelve pieces to @playdeltastrike: eight reels filmed at the booth, two memes and a team photo. The reels used short hooks such as “Want guests who never want to go home?” to turn booth footage into content operators would stop scrolling for.

What I learned

Trade show marketing starts weeks before the doors open. The IAAPA announcement ads got the most reach and the cheapest clicks. The product-specific Delta Matrix ad had the highest click-through rate, at 4.56%, which suggests a clear, visual product hook works well with this audience.

Next time I’d tie every ad and QR code to a booth-specific landing page, so visits and enquiries can be tracked from click to conversation.
