The challenge

Delta Strike sells to an international B2B audience: family entertainment centres, theme parks, trampoline parks and people starting a laser tag business. These buyers research for months before they talk to sales. The site needed to:

  • present a large product range clearly: phasers and vests, arena targets, software, custom arenas, the Delta Matrix LED floor and Delta Putt
  • reach buyers in their own language
  • rank in search, including AI-powered search tools
  • send every enquiry straight to the CRM, and measure which channels bring real leads

What I built

  • Next.js on Vercel: server-rendered pages for speed and SEO, deployed on a global edge network
  • 12 languages from one codebase, with 178 URLs in the sitemap
  • HubSpot CRM integration, plus Delta Strike’s own in-house CRM, so sales sees each lead with context
  • Google Ads conversion tracking and GA4, with a consent banner so non-essential tracking only runs after the visitor agrees
  • Search-ready content: an equipment hub, 15 operator white papers, a “How to start a laser tag business” guide, schema.org structured data and an llms.txt overview for AI search
Paid search result: Google Ads campaigns for high-ticket products average 4 conversions a day.

Beyond the website

The same role covers the rest of Delta Strike’s marketing. That includes trade show collateral for IAAPA, Acolap and Amusement Expo, and paid social. I also worked with TVNZ Breakfast on a news segment featuring the company.
