Dark Horse Coffee · Creative campaign · BDI311 · A-

The Daily Ritual: more than coffee

A story-driven campaign to make Dark Horse Coffee part of people’s daily routines on the Kāpiti Coast. Instead of hard selling, it shows real routines, real people and real moments, building loyalty through habit and emotional connection.

Role
Creative, design, video and email
When
2026 · Yoobee Collaborative Studio
Channels
Instagram, TikTok, Google display, landing page, email, in-store
Tools
Adobe CC, Affinity, DaVinci Resolve, HTML email

The idea

Coffee shoppers are moving towards premium products, and they expect the same great taste every time. The campaign positions Dark Horse as quiet strength, authenticity and consistency: the same great taste every morning, crafted to perfection. It’s a trusted part of the daily ritual, not just another coffee brand.

Three content themes carried the idea across every channel: Work From Dark Horse, Sketch Date with Coffee and A Daily Ritual.

Social

A grid of Instagram posts and a reel, designed to look like real moments in the café rather than adverts.

In store

Table talkers with QR codes that link to the loyalty programme, built around the line “Make every visit count.” They turn a sit-down coffee into a sign-up.

Display & web

A Google display set in leaderboard, rectangle and skyscraper sizes, plus a campaign landing page.

Email

Four HTML emails for the loyalty journey: welcome (with a first coffee on the house), loyalty reward, exclusive promo and monthly update.
